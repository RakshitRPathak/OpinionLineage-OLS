data/tweeteval/   TweetEval sentiment split (Barbieri et al., Findings of EMNLP 2020), derived from SemEval-2017
                  Task 4 (Rosenthal et al., 2017). Copied unchanged from
                  https://github.com/cardiffnlp/tweeteval/tree/main/datasets/sentiment
                  TweetEval is released without restrictions by its authors; restrictions of the original SemEval
                  task and of the X/Twitter terms of service may apply. Cite both papers when reusing it.
                  SHA-256 checksums are listed in data/tweeteval/SHA256SUMS and are verified at run time.
data/streams/     Contaminated opinion streams generated by this repository (stream_generator.py) from the TweetEval
                  texts above. The generated derivative texts, lineage, account and provenance fields are released
                  under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); the embedded tweet texts remain
                  subject to the TweetEval terms above.
